Privacy Policy

EstateLux is designed to help people discover U.S. real-estate listings and manage their property-search experience. This page explains the categories of information the prototype may collect.

Information you provide

When you create an account, you may provide your name, username, email address, phone number and country. When you save properties, searches or submit inquiries, those actions may be associated with your account.

How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate your account, save preferences, process property inquiries, provide notifications and improve the EstateLux experience.

Third-party services

EstateLux may use Supabase for authentication and database services, RentCast for property-data feeds, Google Maps for mapping and a transactional email provider for inquiry and alert delivery. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.
